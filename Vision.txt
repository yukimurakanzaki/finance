# FI Dashboard — Vision

Version: 1.0
Status: Living document

---

# Why this product exists

Most personal finance apps answer questions about the past.

- Where did my money go?
- How much did I spend?
- What's my balance?

Those questions are useful.

They are not the questions people ask before making a purchase.

People ask:

> "Can I afford this today?"

Very few products answer that honestly.

FI Dashboard exists to answer that question with confidence.

---

# Our Mission

Help households make better financial decisions every day.

Not by tracking more data.

Not by producing prettier charts.

But by turning complex financial information into one trustworthy number:

**Safe To Spend Today.**

If that number is trusted,
everything else becomes easier.

---

# The Core Promise

A household should never have to guess whether they can afford something.

Opening the app should immediately answer:

- How much can I safely spend today?
- Am I still on track?
- If I buy this, what changes?

Everything else is secondary.

---

# The Philosophy

## 1. Decisions over bookkeeping

Transactions are history.

Decisions shape the future.

We optimize for tomorrow's decisions, not yesterday's reports.

---

## 2. Simplicity over completeness

We intentionally ignore information that does not improve decisions.

More data is not automatically better.

The best financial dashboard removes uncertainty instead of adding numbers.

---

## 3. Facts over recommendations

The app does not tell users what to do.

It shows the consequences.

Instead of

"Don't buy this."

it says

"This purchase delays your FI date by 11 days."

The decision always belongs to the household.

---

## 4. Savings happen first

Savings are not whatever remains.

Savings are planned before spending begins.

Safe-to-spend is calculated only after savings, obligations, and protected expenses have already been accounted for.

---

## 5. Protected spending stays protected

Some spending should never be optimized away.

Family.

Health.

Education.

Core living expenses.

The app recognizes these as protected categories and never treats them as mistakes.

---

## 6. Financial independence is the destination

Budgeting is not the goal.

Tracking is not the goal.

Net worth is not the goal.

Those are instruments.

The destination is financial independence.

Every feature should help households reach it sooner or with greater confidence.

---

# Design Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of these goals.

✓ Helps users make a decision

✓ Reduces uncertainty

✓ Protects long-term wealth

✓ Saves time

✓ Prevents expensive mistakes

If a feature satisfies none of them,

it should not exist.

---

# Product Hierarchy

The product exists in this order.

## Tier 1

Safe To Spend

Without this, nothing else matters.

---

## Tier 2

Financial Position

- Net Worth
- Cash Position
- Upcoming Obligations

These explain why today's Safe To Spend has that value.

---

## Tier 3

Financial Direction

- FI Projection
- Savings Rate
- Raise Allocation
- Opportunity Cost

These explain where the household is heading.

---

## Tier 4

Bookkeeping

- Transactions
- Categories
- Imports
- Reports
- Assets

Necessary infrastructure.

Not the product.

---

# Success Metric

We do not measure success by:

- number of transactions
- number of reports
- number of categories

We succeed when households open the app before spending money because they trust the answer.

The highest compliment a user can give is:

"I didn't have to think.
I already knew how much I could safely spend."

---

# Product Rule

Every new feature must answer one question before it is built:

"How does this improve the user's confidence in today's spending decision?"

If it doesn't,

it belongs somewhere else.
